## 5. Aggregate MAPE and group size (mape_size.py)

Weekly aggregate MAPE on a group of 27–52 customers is dominated by the sparsity of its
weekly total, so a heavy group's MAPE in §2 cannot be set against the whole light group's.
Scoring the same forecast on 200 random light subsets of the heavy group's size:

| window | model | heavy n | heavy MAPE | light, all | light, random n-matched (median [5%, 95%]) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| electronics 3y | LSTM+bounded32 | 44 (>20) | 84.9 | 38.4 | 128.7 [98.9, 190.6] |
| electronics 3y | ParetoNBD | 44 (>20) | 86.6 | 68.0 | 109.1 [99.1, 133.6] |
| electronics 5y | LSTM+bounded52 | 52 (>20) | 41.4 | 21.1 | 125.7 [90.0, 203.5] |
| electronics 5y | ParetoNBD | 52 (>20) | 41.5 | 29.3 | 125.9 [97.1, 171.9] |
| gift 3y | LSTM+bounded32 | 27 (>10) | 65.1 | 35.6 | 167.6 [115.5, 311.6] |
| gift 3y | ParetoNBD | 27 (>10) | 63.8 | 50.5 | 160.2 [120.3, 257.7] |

At matched size the heavy group is forecast two to four times more accurately at the
weekly level. The positive Δ MAPE entries for >10 and >20 in §2 are a size artefact.
